Explore Dlicom

Dlicom (DLI) is a Web3-native social and communication platform that brings social interaction, secure communication, communities, digital assets, and Web3 tools together in one connected ecosystem.

Built on Base, Dlicom combines familiar social experiences with blockchain-powered functionality, including messaging, communities, a self-custodial wallet, an integrated Web3 browser, and on-chain transactions.

At the center of the ecosystem is $DLI, Dlicom’s native utility token.
